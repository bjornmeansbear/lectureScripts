# Margin map

**A margin map is your prototype drawn small in the middle of a big sheet, with everyone and everything it depends on written in the margins around it.**

Groups of 3–4. Butcher paper, sticky notes, Sharpies, wall space.

The cart was simple, and there was obviously a much larger system around it. Your project lives in a hotel. Same move: zoom out until your prototype is small in the middle.

## Steps

1. **Center.** Put your current prototype in the middle of a big sheet. A sketch, a photo, a sticky note with its name. Small.
2. **Fill the margins.** Who makes it? Who maintains it? Who refills it? Who says no to it? Who's affected outside the revolving door?
3. **Mark every item:** **O** observed · **G** guessed · **?** unknown.
4. **Swap.** The group works each map in turn. What broader systems does this prototype need to think about, engage with, and plan for? Add the hidden actors the maker missed.

## Three prompts for every map

| | |
|---|---|
| **Think about** | Who is affected but never touches it? |
| **Engage with** | Who has to say yes, or take part, for it to work? |
| **Plan for** | What happens over time — maintenance, wear, the next conference? |
