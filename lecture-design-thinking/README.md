**Week 2 — Mon 31 Aug.** Before We Go In: ethnography, ethics, fieldnotes.
Mostly a workshop day — students arrive holding the fifty observations Week 1
assigned, and the day works that pile. The morning opens with about ten minutes
of framing that Week 1 didn't give them: ethnography as a method made of time,
the honest name for the compressed version we're doing, and grounded theory as
the reason the note rules are what they are.

Running order: ethnography → grounded theory → ethics → observations → field
notes → a round outside → what to do with the notes. Affinity and journey maps
are named and explicitly deferred.

- `di200-wk2-deck.html` — 57 slides
- `images/README-wk2.md` — the six image slots and their briefs

**Week 4 — Mon 14 Sep.** Hilton day one: ethics, how to look, the round.
Design borrowed ethnography's method without its ethical structure, and that
gap is the morning's argument, not a list of rules recited at the room. Consent
and the never-record list move to the front of the day, before anyone leaves
the building. Coding used to close this day in 45 minutes; it's Week 5 now.

- `di200-wk4-lecture-script.md`
- `di200-wk4-deck.html` — 18 slides
- `di200-wk4-field-card.md` — one-page field card, carry it, don't read it on site

**Week 5 — Mon 21 Sep.** Coding day. No hotel — the shift from producing field
notes to sifting them for people who weren't there. Sort into O/I/Q, define
what a code is, code the cycle (code → memo → review), six families to code
with, saturation, and codes clustered into patterns by affinity diagramming —
taught as a method this time, not deferred. Ends on pattern statements, one
per group, said out loud to the room.

- `di200-wk5-lecture-script.md`
- `di200-wk5-deck.html` — 30 slides
