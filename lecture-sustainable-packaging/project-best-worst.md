# Best/Worst

Intro assignment, Package Design (GD365) · first run at MICA, Spring 2014 · goes with [Intro to Sustainable Packaging](README.md) · leads into [Worst Re-Design](project-worst-redesign.md) · CC BY-SA 4.0

One week. Assigned on the first day, shown on the second.

## The brief

Go to a store of your choosing (or visit a variety of stores). Pick out two packages: one that you think is the “best” example of a package, and one that you think is the “worst.”

Don't just consider how the package looks. Think about your experience with that package as the consumer: how it catches your eye, how easy it is to get home and open, how the product got to the store in the first place. All of these (and perhaps more) should factor into your decision. Be ready to explain to the class why you chose each one.

## Requirements

1. Choose a best package design.
2. Choose a worst package design.
3. Explain your choices. Have more than a purely superficial or aesthetic reason for each.
4. Bring your examples as JPGs or PDFs.

## Notes for reuse

- The worst package becomes the starting point for [Worst Re-Design](project-worst-redesign.md). Tell students that up front, or don't. Not knowing makes the first pick more honest; knowing makes it more useful.
- In 2014 I wondered whether I should bring my own best/worst examples. Probably yes: it shows what “more than aesthetic” reasoning sounds like.
