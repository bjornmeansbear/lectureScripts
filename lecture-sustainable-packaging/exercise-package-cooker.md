# Package Cooker

In-class exercise, Package Design (GD365) · first run at MICA, Spring 2014 · goes with [Intro to Sustainable Packaging](README.md) · CC BY-SA 4.0

A brainstorming game. About two hours.

## How it works

Draw one **material**, one **structure** and one **thing to package** at random from the lists below. Brainstorm a package that fits. If a draw is too nonsensical, draw again.

1. Break into small groups.
2. Take turns drawing a set of constraints. Spend 10 minutes on ideas: sketching, writing, thinking. Present your best ones back to your group.
3. Do this four or five times. See who comes up with the most interesting ideas.
4. Each person picks their favorite idea and spends about an hour comping it in Illustrator, Photoshop or InDesign.

For one more complication, also draw a **consumer level**. It's also a useful way to think about who the package is for.

The original was a web page that drew for you. Dice, a spreadsheet, or slips of paper in three bowls work just as well.

## The lists

**Material (8):** paperboard, corrugated paperboard, flexible plastic, rigid plastic, glass, metal, wood, composite

**Structure (24):** folding carton, set-top box, canister, tube, tray, sleeve, pouch, bag, blister pack, wrap, cling film, bottle, jar, tub, bowl, drum, barrel, foil, can, aerosol can, beverage can, canning can, crate, hinged-lid tin

**Contents (51):** milk, cereal, Adobe Creative Suite, t-shirt, dress shirt, playing cards, broom, sweater, jeans, frying pan, toothbrush, scissors, chips, pencil, art kit, salt, rice, pens, wine, apples, water, razor, vitamins, vanilla extract, herbs, light bulb, incense, tea, olive oil, soda, smartphone, USB drive, athletic shoes, soap, drink mix, laundry detergent, eggs, ice cream, dress shoes, paint, lip balm, wristwatch, Starburst (or other candy), macaron, bed sheets, nothing, ebook, laptop, tablet, music album, coffee

**Consumer level (3):** high brow (luxury), middle brow (middle of the road), low brow (mass market)

## Notes for reuse

- “Nothing,” “ebook,” “music album” and “Adobe Creative Suite” are deliberate: they set up [Packaging the Immaterial](project-packaging-the-immaterial.md). Swap in current equivalents (a streaming subscription, an app) if the 2014 ones read as dated.
- For a sustainability-focused run, add materials (mycelium, molded pulp, seaweed film, refill/return systems) and a fourth draw: **end of life** (landfill, curbside recycling, compost, return to sender, reuse at home).
