# Intro to Sustainable Packaging (2019)

A talk from February and March 2019: what a package is for (protect, inform, sell), the three layers of packaging, packaging as a boundary, and whether a package needs to exist at all. Built in Scribus, with Flickr Commons images and libre fonts.

Nothing is copied in here yet. The pieces are:

- **Script and deck source.** github.com/bjornmeansbear/sustainable-packaging: `README.md` (the script), the Scribus file (65 slides, 1920×1080), and the final PDFs.
- **Notes, cut into pages.** Tiddlers in `sentence-a-day`, starting from `Intro to Sustainable Packaging` and the hub page `Packaging`.
- **Images.** The Are.na `packaging` channel (`packaging-3dfpkpullxe`). The repo's `links/` folder holds the Flickr images the deck uses, and `Examples-nonlibre/` holds copyrighted examples that shouldn't stay public.
