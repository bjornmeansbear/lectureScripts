# Everything is a Motion Graphic

A GD3 lecture, written and delivered. Until 2026-10-08 this repo listed it as “are.na only, 3 blocks”; the script had been in Google Drive since 2020.

**The argument:** motion is change over time, and graphic design is the relationship between form, content, and context. So a motion graphic is *a change in relationships between form and content and context*. That covers film titles and animated logos, and it also covers a poster people walk past, a billboard seen from a car, and a slit-scan photograph. Everything is a motion graphic.

## Chronology

- **Summer 2020** — Kristian records the lecture as a voice memo. Anna Hanson transcribes it (Drive: “Copy of Intro to Motion - with notes,” June 2020; “Lecture: Wait, What is Motion Graphics?,” July 2020).
- **September 2020** — the edited script, “But Wait, What is ‘Motion Graphics?’,” by Kristian Bjornard, marked CC BY-SA 4.0. Written for GD300 (GD3: Time + Motion + Graphic Design), Fall 2020, taught over Zoom.
- **6 September 2022** — the line “basically… Everything is a motion graphic” is on the week 2 slides for GD300, Fall 2022 (`20220906-NewtonAristotle7RulesOfMotion.pdf`).
- **October 2022** — the proposal with Hayelin Choi for the MODE Summit, “Everything is a Motion Graphic: Expanding the definition of graphic design in motion.” Outcome not recorded.
- **22 October 2022** — a one-word tiddler by this title is created in the wiki and left.
- **8 October 2026** — the script and the proposal are brought into the wiki and this folder.
