Chair-ness(or, what is a chair?)

a designed object for sitting, typically with a seat, back, and often legs.

The essential qualities that make something a chair, regardless of style, material, or context

  1. Function. Supports a seated body.
  2. Structure. A surface to sit on.
  3. Intention. Offers respite.

The rules

  1. One chair per artist, per week.
  2. One work session.
  3. Found and leftover materials only.
  4. Functional and safe to sit in.
  5. Finish and clean up afterward.
  6. Repairs and tweaks allowed.
