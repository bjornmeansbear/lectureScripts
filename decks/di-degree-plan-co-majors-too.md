### What is Design + Innovation? A flexible, transdisciplinary major combining Graphic Design, Product Design, Architectural Design, Social Design, and Interdisciplinary Sculpture. Aligns with MICA’s mission to empower students through creative, purposeful lives and careers. Develops versatile designers equipped to navigate and innovate in a diverse and changing world.
<sub>1775×540 at x=-8761</sub>

- What is Design + Innovation? A flexible, transdisciplinary major combining Graphic Design, Product Design, Architectural Design, Social Design, and Interdisciplinary Sculpture. Aligns with MICA’s mission to empower students through creative, purposeful lives and careers. Develops versatile designers equipped to navigate and innovate in a diverse and changing world.

### Two Pathways for Majors 1. Generally Fine Designer (Self-Directed Major): Breadth of design methods and problem-solving approaches. Core courses + electives from any MICA department (e.g., ESJ, Sculpture, Entrepreneurship). 2. Design Co-Major (Specialized Track): Core courses + specialization in Product, Architectural, or Graphic Design. Underlying shared curriculum fosters collaboration across design fields.
