# Course projects
course-projects · rescuing class project briefs into the lecture folders they go with · updated 2026-11-19

The rule: a brief lives in one place. Briefs for courses being taught now stay in `syllabiBuilder` (the syllabus build includes them), and the lecture folder links to them. Older briefs move here, as `project-<slug>.md` next to their lecture, under CC BY-SA 4.0. Notion's Course Projects database stays the list, and its Source field points at wherever the brief lives.

## Decided
- 2026-11-19 — Briefs go beside their lecture as `project-<slug>.md` (one-session exercises as `exercise-<slug>.md`). No top-level projects folder.
- 2026-11-19 — The split, in Kristian's words: syllabiBuilder is "class stuff, and then governance documents and chair and teacher and academic leadership stuff" (private); lectureScripts is "stuff I am willing to share and make public, lecture content, workshop ideas, etc." So the shareable brief lives here, and the term-specific wrapper (dates, Canvas, rubric, policy) lives there. Current GD420/DI220 briefs stay in syllabiBuilder until their term ends, then move here; the syllabus can include them from here with `<!-- include: ../../lectureScripts/... -->`.
- 2026-11-19 — Slides and site snapshots with other people's images go to `Dropbox/2 Teaching/lectureScripts-files/<folder>/`, not git.
- 2026-11-19 — Notion Source field = the GitHub URL of the brief on `master`. The old URL stays in the page body as “Originally at.”

- 2026-11-19 — Course descriptions and learning outcomes are fine to share too (Kristian). Where and how is still to be worked out case by case. For now they ride along in course-outline files like `lecture-sustainable-packaging/gd365-package-design-2014.md`.
