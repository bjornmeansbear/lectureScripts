Spontaneous Lamp

Each act of making is an act of unmaking. Each design "solving" a problem creates a problem. To counteract this, Spontaneous lamp was created from found, common materials — a 5 gallon bucket, an extension cord, a clip on light, an extra lampshade, loose pebbles and broken bricks, foraged bamboo. Spontaneous lamp supposes that while one might need a lamp right now, one might not tomorrow: so, how can the materials used constructing the lamp return to their original states and continue being raw materials awaiting a next use.
