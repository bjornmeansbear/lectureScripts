SGD: Recycle.

1. There is a REUSE folder to collect reuse exercise files from last weeks homework.

2. Creative Waste Collection. There is also a folder there to collect design "raw materials." These could be images, color palettes, unused vector graphics or layouts, bits of texts you might have written, just other old school projects, whatever kinds of design "raw materials" you can think of that we can remake into new designs. (Mcdonough and Braungart talk about the raw materials of our world that are cycled and recycled over and over again — how can we build ourselves a library of graphic design raw materials to do the same?)

3. Make an aphoristic poster (or zine or whatever works for you) that recycles some of the "Waste" in our shared collection folder. (what kinds of messaging are useful? what can we tell ourselves or a design populace to help them understand what we have been talking about so far?)
(aphoristic poster language is described here by Elliot Earls: https://youtu.be/cRcZT8TtayQ?t=396 — this whole video is pretty great in my opinion, but I don't want you to think you should replicated Earl's 90's avant grade aesthetics — just think about how the language you choose might make for a good poster or not?).
